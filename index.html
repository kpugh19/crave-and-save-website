About Crave & Save

Crave & Save is an independent recipe, meal-planning, and grocery comparison app currently being developed in the United States.

Our mission is simple: help people spend less time organizing meals and more time enjoying them, while finding practical ways to save on everyday shopping.

We are currently developing and testing the application and exploring authorized partnerships with grocery, household, and retail brands.

Retailer & Affiliate Partnerships

Crave & Save is exploring partnerships with retailers and affiliate networks to connect shoppers with relevant products and purchasing options.

We aim to compare shopping options fairly, including retailers that do not offer affiliate commissions. Product pricing and availability will depend on authorized retailer data and integrations.

Affiliate disclosure: When affiliate shopping links become available, Crave & Save may earn a commission on qualifying purchases at no additional charge through the affiliate link itself. Affiliate relationships will be disclosed to users.

Retailers and affiliate networks interested in working with Crave & Save are welcome to contact us.
